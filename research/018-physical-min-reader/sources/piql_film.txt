When digital storage fails, piqlFilm endures. Photosensitive Silver Halide Technology piqlFilm combines cutting-edge photosensitive technology with the proven longevity of film. Your data is encoded at microscopic resolution (1180 line pairs per mm) onto nano silver halides on ultra-stable PET (polyethylene terephthalate) base material, creating a digital physical backup impervious to technological obsolescence, electromagnetic pulses, and data degradation. The piqlFilm accommodates all digital information types and formats without discrimination. Databases, video files, photographs, documents, and audio recordings can all be preserved with equal fidelity on this versatile storage medium. As a write-once-read-many (WORM) storage solution, piqlFilm serves the critical need for archiving valuable information requiring safe storage across multiple decades. Get in touch with us to learn more about the scientific testing that is done. Quick-facts What is the max data capacity? The piqlFilm has a total storage capacity of 120GB per film. This equates to approximately 65,000 frames of film. How is the data stored? Data can be stored in various formats on the 35mm piqlFilm, including digital (QR codes) and visual (images). A combination of both formats can also be used. What material is the film made out of? PET (polyethylene terephthalate) base coated with gelatin emulsion containing silver halides. 100% recyclable. Can it withstand environmental disasters? Tested against extreme conditions, piqlFilm maintains integrity in environments where digital systems fail completely. What if the reading technology becomes obsolete? The read-back process is fully documented, ensuring future readability through open specifications. The piqlFilm is stored in the piqlBox.
