# Microform Duplication Services

Microfilm reels created through the UW Libraries Microfilming Program can be duplicated by:

- Self-service scanning in Government Publications, Maps, Microforms & Newspapers 
- Backstage Library Works duplication service

Titles listed in the UW Microfilm Print Masters Inventory are available for purchase through Backstage Library Works in microfilm and digital formats. 

Microfilm may also be borrowed through Interlibrary Loan & Scanning Services.

## Ask Us!

Get help from librarians by email, phone, 24/7 chat, or make an appointment with a subject expert

## Duplicates in Microfilm Format

- Duplicate 35mm positive and/or negative microfilm reels are produced on Fuji silver halide polyester duplicating film.
- Most people order positive microfilm.
- $58.75 per reel for order processing plus delivery.

## Duplicates in Digital Format

The Reel Deal

- 8-bit grayscale TIFF images for archive.
- 8-bit grayscale JPEG compressed derivative images for access.
- Single page PDFs, one per TIFF (with uncorrected OCR).
- One digital image per film frame, reflecting single pages or two-page spreads, as filmed. 
- Files will be named using the microfilm reel number followed by a sequential counter.
- $89.00 per reel. There may be an additional charge for Reel Deal file delivery. 

The Reel Deal Plus with PDF option

- Two-up frames are split to single page TIFFs.
- Thumbnail review to flag breaks, such as the first page of each issue.
- Metadata-based, intuitive file directory structure.
- Metadata-based file names (title, year, month, day, sequential page counter)
- Searchable multi-page PDFs with uncorrected OCR-bound per segment. For example: one PDF per newspaper issue.
- $89.00 per reel plus $0.12 per page. There may be an additional charge for Reel Deal file delivery.

## Payment & Delivery

Payment and delivery are handled by Backstage Library Works:

- Individuals in the United States and individuals and institutions outside of the United States must pre-pay.
- Requesting individual / institution must pay packing and shipping charges. This may include the shipment of microfilm from UW Libraries to Backstage as needed. 
- Orders will be shipped via UPS with the option of rush and overnight shipping.
- There may be an additional charge for Reel Deal file delivery. 

- Backstage requests that customers send a USB drive or hard drive or purchase one through Backstage. 
- Depending on the data size of the project, Backstage may be able to deliver files via a cloud-based file transfer service. 

## To Order

- Search the UW Microfilm Print Masters Inventory to see if an item is available for duplication. 
- Using the information listed in the UW Microfilm Print Masters Inventory, fill out the Microfilm Duplication Request form.
- Once we receive your order, we will retrieve the microfilm and ship it to Backstage Library Works to be duplicated. We will inform you if your order cannot be filled.
- Backstage Library Works will handle all payments and ship the items to you. 
- Questions? Email mcnews@uw.edu.
