Rob Kairis, "Comparing gifts to purchased materials: a usage study", Library Collections, Acquisitions, and Technical Services 24(3), 2000. (Kent State University Stark Campus Library, close to 80,000 volumes)
抜粋（原文どおり、Conclusions の冒頭の公開部分）:
"The fact that the library used in the study was a small academic library with highly selective procedures for adding donated materials (only 18% of the materials given to the library during the 1996/1997 academic year were added to its collection) lends credibility to the contention that gifts are used less than purchased items."
